Refund & Cancellation

A public baseline; the signed project agreement controls project-specific commercial terms.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Before work starts

Cancellation before project work begins is handled according to the written quotation or agreement. Third-party charges that have already been committed may be non-refundable.

2. After work begins

For custom development, design, content or marketing work, any refund is considered against the scope already completed, committed costs, third-party purchases and the terms of the signed agreement.

3. Deposits and milestone payments

Where a project uses deposits or milestones, the exact treatment of those payments should be stated in writing before work starts.

4. Ad spend and third-party tools

Advertising media spend, domains, hosting, subscriptions, premium assets and other third-party fees are generally not controlled by WEBORA once purchased or committed and may not be refundable by the third party.

5. Requests

Send cancellation or refund requests to weborastudiozz@gmail.com with the project name and reason. We will review the request against the applicable written terms.

Project-specific terms: Refunds and cancellations are handled according to the applicable written project terms.
